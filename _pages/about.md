I invite you to explore my [data science portfolio](/portfolio/), which is continually growing. It currently features [pyNamo-EGT](/portfolio/pynamo/), my Python package for analysing and visualising evolutionary game dynamics, and a [pre-match analysis](/portfolio/shelton-alcaraz/) of the Shelton–Alcaraz 2026 US Open quarter-final.
